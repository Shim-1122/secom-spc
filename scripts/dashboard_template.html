SECOM fab sensor monitoring

Statistical process control on the UCI SECOM dataset: . Built from the pipeline exports in Shim-1122/secom-spc. Hover any chart for values.

Line-test pass rate by day

Daily pass rate and its 7-day rolling mean. The vertical line marks the change point detected from the sensors alone, without using the pass/fail labels.

Failed runs per day

Days above the Laney p′ upper limit (which corrects the p chart for overdispersion) are marked.

A PCA model of the sensors is fitted on passing runs before the change point. Hotelling T² measures unusual levels along the normal correlation pattern; SPE measures departures from that pattern. Limits are the empirical 99th percentile of out-of-fold reference scores.

SPE (squared prediction error) by run

SPE steps up at the change point and stays above the frozen limit. Failed runs are not higher than passing runs: the monitor sees a sensor change, not product failure.

Hotelling T² by run

T² moves much less than SPE, so the correlation structure changed more than the operating level did.

Multivariate alarms per day under three limit strategies

Frozen limits come from Phase I and are never updated. Re-baselined limits are refitted on 250 passing runs after the change, so they apply from the start of the monitoring period. Rolling limits are refitted daily on the last 150 passing runs.

Operating figures in the tiles cover the monitoring period only, after the re-baselining window. No strategy flags failed runs at a higher rate than passing runs.

Sensors behind the SPE shift

Share of total SPE carried by each sensor, before and after the change point. Frozen-then-live sensors reported a held value for weeks, then began reporting live readings.

Top failure-associated sensors

Single-sensor AUC for the 15 sensors with the best consensus rank, drawn as distance from chance (0.5), on the training period and on later hold-out data. Bars that switch sides did not hold up.

Individuals control chart

Limits are estimated from Phase I and applied unchanged to Phase II. Marked points break at least one of the eight Nelson rules.

Data: UCI SECOM (1,567 runs, 2008). Sensor names and units are anonymised, so no physical interpretation is possible. Tables: CSV exports.
